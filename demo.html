See how our 14-step Tax Waterfall algorithm automatically selects the optimal lots to minimize your capital gains taxes.

How It Works

The Tax Waterfall processes each purchase through 14 decision logic steps:

  • Apply carry-forward losses to gains
  • Harvest up to $3,000 OID deduction
  • Balance gains vs losses for tax neutrality
  • Accumulate losses for future years
  • Prioritize long-term gains (lower rate)
